Sobre mí

I'm Max Sanchez Luckert. I've spent 15+ years inside growing companies, from digital media and advertising to agriculture tech, building teams, fixing operations, and helping owners through messy growth. I started at Deloitte in 2010 and have run my own consulting practice since 2016. I'm a generalist on purpose: I connect the people side, the money side, and the way work gets done, because in a growing company they break together. I also lean hard on AI. It makes a one-person practice move faster than it has any right to. I work in English, French, and Spanish, and hold my own in Italian and Portuguese. Forty-plus countries and one open water diving license later, I'm at home in most rooms.
